FAQ

Questions contractors ask

Is QuoFast free?

Yes, to start. Create as many quotations and invoices as you like — your first 3 documents are free to send. After that, Pro gives you unlimited sending.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.

Do I need an SSM-registered company?

No. Freelancers and one-person contractors use QuoFast too. SST settings are optional.

Can I put my own logo on the documents?

Yes. Upload your logo once and it appears on every quotation and invoice.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted in transit and never sold. No ads, no tracking. You can delete your account and all its data at any time.

How do I cancel Pro?

Any time in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for.
