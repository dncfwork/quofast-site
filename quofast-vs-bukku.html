QuoFast vs Bukku

These two are often compared, but they are not the same kind of tool. Bukku is full accounting software. QuoFast is a fast quotation and invoice app. Here is how to tell which one you actually need.

  • Both made for Malaysia
  • Honest comparison
  • No jargon

Both are Malaysian and both can produce an invoice, so they turn up in the same searches — but they aim at different jobs. Bukku is proper cloud accounting software: it keeps your books, your inventory and your tax position. QuoFast is a quotation and invoice app: it makes a clean, professional document on your phone in under a minute and sends it on WhatsApp. Picking right starts with one question — do you need to keep accounts, or do you need to get a quote out?

Choose QuoFast if…

You are a one-person business, a contractor or a freelancer, and the paperwork you dread is the quotation and the invoice — not the accounts. You want to stand at the job, pick the client, list the work and send a tidy PDF on WhatsApp before you have left the driveway. You do not want to learn an accounting system, and you do not (yet) need to file e-invoices with LHDN. QuoFast is built for exactly that, in your language, with SST and your SSM number already in place, for RM 29.90 a month.

Choose Bukku if…

You need real books. Bukku is a genuinely strong Malaysian accounting platform — it tracks purchases and inventory, reconciles your bank, produces SST-02 and more than fifty reports, and submits validated e-invoices to LHDN's MyInvois. If your business is past the point where a quote-and-invoice app is enough — you have stock, staff, or an accountant asking for proper statements — that is what Bukku is for, and QuoFast does not try to replace it.

Honest bottom line

This is not really a fight. A lot of small operators start with QuoFast because the first thing a new business has to do is quote and get paid, and move to — or add — a full accounting tool like Bukku later, when keeping books becomes the job. Use the lightest tool that does what today actually needs. If that is sending a professional quotation this afternoon, QuoFast is free to try.
